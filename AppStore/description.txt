Lane Weaver is a local-first arcade motorcycle traffic-dodger. Weave through dense highway traffic, chain clean passes, and chase your own distance records on Windows with keyboard or gamepad, and on phones and tablets with touch controls.

Planned features:
- Three original routes: a desert canyon, a neon tunnel, and a coastal city.
- Traffic that gets faster and denser the longer you survive.
- Clean-pass score multipliers for close, well-timed lane changes.
- Personal best records per route, stored only on your device, with JSON export and restore.
- Options for reduced motion, flash limiting, remappable controls, scalable text, and high contrast.
- Optional tilt steering on mobile, always with a touch or button alternative.

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network connection required.

Status: in development. This description lists planned features and will be updated as each one ships.
